# Komica Reader

A userscript that turns Komica's 綜合 board (`gaia.komica1.org/00b/`, including its category views such as 動畫 or 新聞), the boards on 2cat (`2cat.org`, `2cat.uk`) and the desktop portal at `komica1.org` into a three-column reader in the style of a macOS mail app: boards on the left, threads in the middle, the thread you are reading on the right.

- **Threads are a list.** Each row shows a thumbnail, the title (an untitled thread just shows its text), three lines, the time in the corner, and the reply count. Replies stay folded; **展開最新 N 則** peeks at the latest ones in the row. Click a row, its thumbnail, or a `>>No.` link into another thread to read it. **j** and **k** move through the list.
- **The thread opens at once.** The OP and the replies the board already shows appear immediately. The rest of the thread is fetched in the background and filled in. The row after the one you open is fetched ahead. Images and videos are shown at full size, up to 70% of the window height; click an image to see it full screen. Media the poster marked `[hidepix]` stays blurred until clicked. Hover `>>No.` to preview that post; click it to jump there. Each post lists who replied to it. At 900px and wider the pane opens on the right when you choose a thread; drag its left edge to resize it, and × or Esc closes it so the list takes the whole width again. Narrower, it covers the list and **‹ 列表** goes back. 2cat shows a long thread a page at a time and opens on its last page; **載入全部** fetches every page and draws the whole thread.
- **The form comes to you.** The site's own post form floats instead of sitting at the top of the page. With a thread open, it is a reply bar at the bottom of the pane; **No.** on any post quotes it there. **✎ 發新串** in the toolbar switches it to a new thread. Esc folds it; Esc again closes the thread. Something already written stays with the thread it was written for: opening or closing another thread does not move it. **回覆** or **No.** in another thread moves it there, and so does emptying the box.
- **One board sidebar, on the boards and in the portal.** Search (press **/**), **★ 書籤**, the last six boards under **最近**, and folding categories. Boards that were unreachable at the last survey stay in their category, dimmed and tagged 連不上. The 18禁 section asks for a birth year first, the same check the portal makes. In the portal's `menu` frame the sidebar uses the portal's live links and opens a board as the page itself, with its own sidebar: the portal's right-hand frame is an old-style `<frame>`, and a board from another address inside it cannot take its videos fullscreen (the browser greys the button). A board found in a frame like that anyway says so at the top, with a link that opens it as the page.
- **Light and dark.** The look follows the system appearance while the site theme is 預設, and the site's 夜間 theme when it is chosen in the toolbar's **⋯** menu, which also holds the site's own header links.
- **The frame first.** While a page is still arriving, the reader's frame shows at once: on a board (and the next page of one) the sidebar, a bar with the board's name and a spinner; on a thread page the bar and the spinner. The site's page underneath, half laid out, stays covered until the reader has it, or for ten seconds at most.

## Rules the design keeps

- **The site's own form is the form.** Komica's form uses random field names, honeypot fields and a Cloudflare Turnstile check, so the script never builds its own POST. It makes the existing form float, never moves, clones or hides it (moving it would reload the Turnstile frame and drop its token), and replies by adding a `resto` field to it, as the site's own Quick Reply does. The form posts into a hidden frame, so the list and the thread stay put. The text in the box is cleared only once the post itself is found, on the page the form landed on or in the refetched thread: a post newer than any in the thread when it was sent, holding the text that was sent. Anything else, including a result page that never arrives and a reply that is only an image or only quotes (nothing tells it from anyone else's), is reported as "看不出有沒有送出" and the text stays.
- **Post text is copied, not trusted.** The pane draws posts from the thread page's markup. Scripts, frames, forms, SVG and the like are dropped on the way, only a few attributes survive (links, images, classes and font colors), and a link or image has to point to `http` or `https` as the browser itself reads the address.
- **The site's ▲▼ are the site's.** The pane draws each post's slot with the site's own classes and number, and the site's click handler sends the vote, so voting keeps whatever the site does: its pause, its identity, its answer. Your vote shows as the site marks it (▲ red, ▼ blue), so a post you have voted on looks it. While the site has paused voting, its notice (系統忙碌，投票功能暫停) keeps the site's warning colours, above the folded reply form on a phone, and the ▲▼ it has turned off look off, in the pane too. Votes need the site's own cookies, which the browser withholds from a page framed by another site (2cat.uk frames its boards): the sidebar opens a board on another site as the page itself, and a 綜合 board or thread found inside another site's frame says so at the top, with a link that opens it as the page (Chrome and Safari, which tell a frame what site it is in; not Firefox). A post the answer folds folds in the pane; the reader's own 隱藏設定 on the site (IDs, keywords, the 隱藏貼文清單) hide replies in the pane as they do on the board.
- **The site's other per-post tools come the same way** (綜合 only; 2cat has none of them). Each post in the pane carries the site's ▾ (檢舉、隱藏、加入圖書館／映畫館／備忘錄、複製) and, beside its ID, the site's ID menu (搜尋此 ID、隱藏此 ID), as buttons the site's own scripts pick up. The ▾ reads its post from the page around it, so before it opens in the pane the reader wraps it, hidden, in the shape the site's page has (thread and post numbers, name, time, text, thumbnail). Its 類別 are links to the site's category pages, opening in a new tab. Hiding from either menu applies in the pane at once; Esc shuts a site menu without closing the thread.
- **IDs as the site used to show them.** Once the whole thread is in, each ID says how many posts it has here (×N, or 單發) and is coloured by that; clicking an ID marks its posts and dims the rest.
- **The site's own lines are set, not dropped.** The list rows keep the site's 類別 and ▾; the form keeps the site's 回應模式 line, its notices (folded behind one line) and its 發文說明與注意事項; the pager and the footer keep every link and line. The site's floating ▲▼ (to the page's top and bottom, not votes), and 2cat's ↑ ⟳ ↓ on a phone, are round grey buttons, clear of the floating form. Beside an open thread they keep to the list's edge and scroll the list; on a phone, where the thread covers the list, they take the thread to its start or end. The site's ads are cards of their own marked 廣告, where the site puts them (綜合's wide-screen or phone banner, as the site picks it), never floated beside a row (the column 2cat's older boards keep beside the list is a card above it); a slot the ad network left empty is not shown. Still hidden: 2cat's second list of the page's threads, the board's delete form, and in list rows the file line, the ID and the [回應] link (the pane has them).
- **What the pane does not have** is the site's five-minute edit. **↗** opens the thread in a new tab, where it is.

## Install

1. Install a userscript manager: [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) or [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/). In Chrome 138 and later, also turn on **Allow user scripts** on the manager's page in `chrome://extensions`.
2. Open [komica-reader.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/operonlab/userscripts/main/komica-reader/komica-reader.user.js) and accept the install.

On a phone, use a userscript manager for the phone's browser: Tampermonkey or Macaque for Safari on iOS, Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey in Firefox for Android. The narrow layout (thread over the list, sidebar as an overlay, the form across the bottom) has been checked in an emulated iPhone, not yet on a device.

## Limits

- The reader runs on `gaia.komica1.org/00b/` and on 2cat's boards, both its older layout (職業) and its newer one (手機遊戲). Links to Komica's other boards open those boards' own pages; they run other software on other hosts.
- **載入全部** on a long 2cat thread asks the server for every page, four at a time: a thread of 760 replies is 26 pages.
- Speed has a floor: the board's server takes 0.7 to 1.9 s to send a thread page (measured 2026-10-27). The script shows what it already has at once, but the full thread cannot arrive sooner than the server sends it.
- Stars, recent boards, folded categories and the 18禁 answer are shared across hosts through Tampermonkey's `GM_getValue`/`GM_setValue`; without them each host keeps its own.
- Posting has not been tested: the end-to-end test never submits. It checks that the form floats, takes the quote, carries the thread's `resto` and the Turnstile widget, and targets the hidden result frame.
- The site changes often: in October 2026 its scripts were updated almost daily. If something looks wrong after a site update, turn the script off for that tab; the board itself still works.

## Tests

- `node --test test/` runs the unit tests: links, quoting, widths, reply counts and times, keyboard steps, board URLs and search, the age check, media kinds and sizes, and how a post's result page is read.
- `node research/build-boards.mjs` refreshes the embedded board list from `research/survey-result.json`; add `--check` to fail if the list is stale.
- `node test/e2e.mjs [out-dir]` runs the live check in headless Chromium against the real 綜合 board and two 2cat boards, using the `playwright` package. Set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` to the package path if it cannot be resolved, and `KR_CHROMIUM` to a browser executable if Playwright's own build is not downloaded. It exits `0` pass, `1` a check failed, `2` could not check.
